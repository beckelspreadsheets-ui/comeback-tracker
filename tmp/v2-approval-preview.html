4 · What changed in this pass (your feedback: "cheap boxy minecraft")

The karts and item boxes are now professionally-authored 3D models, not code-stacked boxes: the open-wheel racer and Mario-style item box from Kenney's Toy Car Kit (CC0 public domain — free for commercial use, no attribution needed; license file shipped in-repo, recorded in the asset manifest). Each kart is recolored at runtime to your V2 palette — hero red, purple/blue/orange rivals — by remapping the model's paint swatches while keeping the baked shading. Earlier passes: real-time shadows, neon bloom, asphalt/grass textures, MK camera, toon shading. FPS went up to 40–44 (authored kart = 5 meshes vs ~45 procedural). The kit also includes bananas, coins, cones, gates, and more vehicles — a ready-made roster/items upgrade path.
