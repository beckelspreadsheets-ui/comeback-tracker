Reset your progress

Race results, best times, settings and replay ghosts are saved only on your device. To start over:

  • iPhone or iPad: delete the app, then install it again.
  • Web browser: clear this site’s data in your browser’s settings.

This can’t be undone. We never get a copy of your progress, so we can’t restore it.

Questions

Where are the controls explained?
In the game’s How to Race guide. It opens the first time you play, and you can bring it back from the menu with the How to race & item guide button.
How do I drift?
Going into a corner, flick your thumb quickly toward the turn and keep it on the screen, or hold the Drift button. Sparks build from blue to gold to purple; lift your thumb or let go to fire the boost. The longer you hold the drift, the bigger the boost. On a keyboard, hold Space; on a controller, hold RB.
How do I turn motion steering on or off?
During a race, tap Tilt in the top-right corner. The button shows Tilt on or Tilt off. It starts off, and the game remembers your choice on that device. If you’re asked to allow motion access, choose Allow, or Tilt stays off. Your thumb always takes over while it’s on the screen.
Do I need an internet connection?
Only for the first load. After that, Penguin Kart works offline.
What happens to my data?
Your progress stays on your device. The game has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and sends no personal data anywhere. The privacy policy has the details.
