Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026 · Published by Showcase Designs

The short version

  • No accounts
  • No ads
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No personal data sent anywhere

Penguin Kart doesn’t collect information about you. What the game saves, it saves on your device.

What stays on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves your race results, best times, settings and replay ghosts in local storage on your device: the app’s own storage on iPhone and iPad, or your browser’s storage when you play on the web. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else, and we can’t see it.

Deleting it

Delete the app from your iPhone or iPad, or clear this site’s data in your browser, and everything the game saved is gone. We never receive a copy, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Tilt steering

If you switch on Tilt, the game reads your device’s motion sensors to steer your kart. Those readings are used on the spot and are never saved or sent anywhere. Tilt stays off until you turn it on.

Offline and on the web

Penguin Kart works offline after the first load.

If you play in a web browser, that first load comes from our web host. Like any website, it receives your IP address and basic browser details so it can deliver the game’s files. The game adds nothing about you to those requests.

Purchases

There are no in-app purchases yet.

Changes to this policy

A future update may add an optional feature that turns a picture you choose into your own custom racer. It will only arrive with an updated version of this policy, and it will ask for your explicit consent first.

Whenever this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.
