Launch Lab — the "flying in the air" bug (K2.5)

"you go through one of the turns left then you go up a ramp and all of a sudden you go flying in the air and it doesn't show you the ramp that you're going up and over … it does it twice on you and so you just look like you're flying in the air so it looks super weird."

What's actually happening (reproduced + measured): Comeback City has THREE scripted launch spots. At race speed a launch throws the kart ~330 world units over ~1.3 seconds — the ramp that caused it is out of frame in a fraction of a second, and there is no landing marker anywhere, so mid-air there is zero visual explanation. The "does it twice" is real and it's every lap: the bridge-crest launch (right after the left carousel + climb) and ramp B fire ~2.4 seconds apart on the normal racing line. Bonus problem: the launch trigger is ~50% wider than the visible ramp, so you can fly having visibly missed the wedge.

The fix menu — pick per site (one variable per change, each lands separately)

(a) Trigger matches the visual — you only launch if you visibly drove onto the wedge (tighten rampHitLane 0.22 → 0.15). Fairness fix, kills the "launched from nowhere" cases. Applies: ramps A + B.
(b) Landing-zone marker — a glowing ring / chevron strip on the road where the flight puts you down, visible from the launch. The single biggest "oh, this is a JUMP" signal mid-air. Applies: all three sites.
(c) Bigger ramp + approach chevrons — beefier wedge, painted arrows on the asphalt leading in, so the cause reads BEFORE you hit it. Applies: ramps A + B.
(d) Crest jump-gate — replace the thin strip with a full-width, unmissable jump-gate dressing at the bridge top (glow arch / launch lights), so the big launch has a big cause. Applies: crest.
(e) Shorten the flights — trim launch power so airtime drops (feel change — needs your explicit sign-off, changes how the jumps play). Last resort.
(remove) — any site can just be deleted instead (say which).
