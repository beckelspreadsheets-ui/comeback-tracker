# Ordinal holder permission — message template

Penguin Kart uses the group's ordinals as racers, rivals, statues, spectators
and Penguin March marchers. Before a public TestFlight link or the App Store,
get a written yes from each holder whose character appears. A reply to this
message in DM or email is enough to keep on file; save a screenshot of each
answer. This is a plain-language template, not legal advice.

**Characters in the game today:** CRRT Bunny, Lifoladen, T Clow, Seth Penguin,
Mizzle, Layer 23 (racers and rivals); the penguin ordinals among them also
appear as Penguin Village statues, spectators and in the Penguin March item.

---

**Message (copy, fill in the brackets, send):**

> Hey [name]! I'm putting Penguin Kart on the App Store (iPhone and iPad) under
> my company, Showcase Designs. Your ordinal [character / inscription number]
> is one of the racers.
>
> Can I have your permission to use it in the game and to promote the game?
> That covers:
> - the character's name, artwork and the 3D racer we made from it, inside the
>   game (racing, menus, statues, spectators, items);
> - screenshots, the trailer, the App Store page and social posts about the
>   game;
> - the free game now and future updates, including paid features.
>
> You keep owning your ordinal; this just lets me use it in Penguin Kart. I'll
> credit you in the game's credits as [handle]. If you ever want it removed,
> tell me and I'll take it out of the next update.
>
> A quick "yes, I agree" reply is all I need. Thanks!
