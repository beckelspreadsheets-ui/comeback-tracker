# Inscription Circuit — Art Direction (M1)

**Status:** M1 visual thesis + graybox basis. This document is the anti-Comeback-City /
anti-Penguin-Village contract: every visual decision below is deliberately different
from both retired maps.

## Visual thesis

A twilight **spaceport–harbor–mesa** world where three Ordinal inscriptions are
literally carved into the landscape: a circuit etched into dark desert stone,
glowing like fresh inscription ink. The three supplied characters are not guests
in someone else's city — each one *owns a district* of a single cohesive fantasy
world. The read at racing speed: **teal runway light → gold lantern light →
violet dusk signal light**, three distinct lighting languages on one lap.

Nothing carries over: no neon-deco city towers, no Miami pastels, no snow/ice,
no alpine cabins, no penguin statues, no ₿-coin-shaped city furniture language
(coins remain as a mechanic only, restyled in M4/M5).

## The three districts

### 1. Launch Yard (isethius — astronaut) — progress 0.00–0.30
- **Fantasy:** a small private spaceport at dusk, built around one launch strip.
- **Silhouette:** long horizontal runway lines, low gantries, big satellite dishes
  tilted at the sky, a squat mission-control tower.
- **Light language:** teal/cyan strip lighting (`#2ee6c8`) with warm orange
  runway markers (`#ff8b21`) — pulled straight from isethius's teal visor and
  orange hoodie.
- **Road read:** widest section of the lap, gentle fast sweep, luminous edge strips.

### 2. Blackflag Wharf (t clow — pirate cat) — progress 0.30–0.62
- **Fantasy:** a crooked timber freeport at night — shipyard switchbacks between
  moored hulls, crate stacks, mast forests.
- **Silhouette:** vertical masts + triangular sails and black flags against the
  sky, broken horizontal boardwalk edges, crane jibs.
- **Light language:** lantern gold (`#ffb23e`) against black timber (`#171019`)
  with blood-red flag accents (`#d43a2e`) — from t clow's hat band.
- **Road read:** the technical section — narrowest road, tight left–right
  switchbacks, no center dashes (boardwalk planks, not asphalt).

### 3. Layer23 Mesa (Layer23 — wide-brim hat) — progress 0.62–0.92
- **Fantasy:** a high-desert observatory mesa at violet dusk; stone arches and
  signal beacons built by someone who watches the chain from far away.
- **Silhouette:** one big mesa mass the road climbs and descends, natural stone
  arches straddling the road, thin beacon spires with gold lights.
- **Light language:** purple dusk (`#8a5fd0`) with gold beacon fire (`#ffd34f`)
  — from Layer23's purple hoodie and hat-band red-gold.
- **Road read:** the vertical section — climb, crest jump (signature airtime),
  fast descent back toward the coast.

Global palette: deep indigo ground/stone (`#241c2e`), one shared dusk sky
(indigo → teal → burnt-orange horizon), districts separated by *light color and
silhouette*, not by separate skies.

## Top-down track diagram (new topology — not a rearrangement of either old map)

```
                 N (−z)
        W7 ──────────────W6
       /  boardwalk       \ switchback
      W8                   W5──┐
      |                       │  ↑ Blackflag Wharf
      W9  mesa climb          W4
      |                        \
      W10  crest/descent        W3  opening sweep
       \                        │  ↑ Launch Yard
        W1 ═══════════════════ W2
           launch straight (start/finish near W1), travel W1→W2→…→W1
```

Macro silhouette: **fast eastern opening sweep → technical northern wharf
pinch → western mesa climb → signature southern descent + risk/reward cut**
back onto the launch straight. Distinct from Comeback City (dense city grid
loop with central bridge) and Penguin Village (single dented oval).

Validated geometry (graybox): ~2,750 world units, min corner radius ~65
(wharf switchbacks), no self-intersections.

## Landmark sheet (graybox masses in M1, dressed in M3)

1. **Mission Control + dish farm** (Launch Yard, outside of W2–W3) — squat
   tower + 3 tilted dishes; first landmark after the start.
2. **Runway gantry arch** (start/finish) — teal truss gate.
3. **The Blackflag** (Wharf, inside W5) — one big moored pirate hull with
   black sail; the wharf's hero mass.
4. **Crate-and-mast forest** (Wharf edges) — repeated vertical rhythm.
5. **Mesa arch** (straddles the road at ~0.70) — the climb's gate.
6. **Signal beacon spire** (mesa crest) — gold light visible from most of the
   lap; doubles as the crest-jump aim point.
7. **The Inscription slab** (final cut inside) — glowing carved stone slab
   that marks the shortcut entrance.

## Road language

- One dark stone-asphalt family everywhere; districts differ by **edge
  language**: teal glow strips (Launch Yard), timber plank shoulders with no
  center line (Wharf), gold-lit curb stones (Mesa).
- Barriers: low dark stone walls with district-colored light caps — not CC's
  red/white city barriers, not PV's ice walls.
- Curbs: orange/white in Launch Yard, gold/dark in Wharf, violet/white in Mesa.

## Camera & speed (targets, tuned M2/M5)

- Slightly lower, closer chase framing than CC's tall read; road occupies more
  of the lower frame; crest jump reveals the whole descent + coast.
- Speed feel from edge-strip parallax (runway lights, plank seams, curb
  stones), not from city window grids or snowfall.

## Explicitly rejected (rejection evidence)

- Any CC neon-deco tower, Miami pastel, GYM/FOOD/LAB/CLINIC/GARAGE district,
  the green-park ground, the magenta-amber sky.
- Any PV snow/ice/aurora, igloos, penguin statues, fish-market dressing.
- Nintendo trade dress of any kind.
