# Free Website Review Template

Status: delivery template for the Showcase Designs v3 launch offer.

Use this for every free review so the offer stays specific, truthful, and consistent. The review should help the business even if they never buy. Do not include a quote unless the business asks for one.

Every delivered review must include 3 trust or conversion issues, 3 local SEO opportunities, and 1 recommended next step.

Write 3-5 plain sentences.

No rankings are guaranteed. Local SEO depends on competition, relevance, distance, prominence, reviews, fresh photos, accurate business details, and proof of completed work.

## Review Quality Rules

- Be specific to the business. Do not send generic audits.
- Do not invent traffic, rankings, revenue, or lead volume.
- Do not claim the business is losing calls unless the evidence supports that wording.
- Do not diagnose paid ads, legal, medical, or financial issues.
- Do not promise rankings, map pack placement, traffic, calls, or revenue.
- Do not recommend Growth if the business will not provide reviews, photos, proof, accurate details, or GBP access.
- If the site is already strong, say so and identify smaller improvements.
- Keep the review short enough that a busy owner can read it in under five minutes.
