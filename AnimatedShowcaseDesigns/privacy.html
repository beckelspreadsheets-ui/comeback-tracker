Who We Are

What We Collect

When you fill out our contact form, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Business type
  • Package preference (Starter, Growth, or Dominate)
  • Your message (free-text)

That's it. We don't collect anything else automatically—no IP logging, no device fingerprinting, no tracking pixels.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit through our contact form for one purpose: to respond to your inquiry and discuss how we can help your business. We may follow up by email or phone based on the contact details you provide.

Form Processing

Our contact form is processed by FormSubmit.co, a third-party form handling service. When you submit the form, your data passes through their servers and is forwarded to us via email. FormSubmit.co's own privacy practices are governed by their privacy policy.

Cookies & Analytics

We do not use cookies. We do not run any analytics, tracking scripts, or third-party advertising on this website. Your visit is not tracked.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with anyone. The only third party that processes your data is FormSubmit.co, as described above, and solely for the purpose of delivering your form submission to us.

Data Retention

We keep your contact form submissions in our email inbox for as long as needed to respond to your inquiry and maintain an ongoing business relationship. If you'd like us to delete your information, just email us and we'll take care of it.

Your Rights (CCPA)

If you're a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we've collected about you
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out of the sale of your personal information (we don't sell it, but you still have this right)
  • Non-discrimination—we won't treat you differently for exercising your rights

To exercise any of these rights, email us at andrew@showcase-designs.com. We'll respond within 45 days.

Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed at anyone under 16. We don't knowingly collect information from minors.

Changes to This Policy

If we make meaningful changes to this policy, we'll update the date at the top of the page. For significant changes, we may also note the update on our homepage.
