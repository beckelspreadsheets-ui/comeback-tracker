# Client Onboarding Requirements

Status: required sales and onboarding guardrail for Showcase Designs v3 launch clients.

Use this before quoting Growth or any local SEO-heavy scope. The goal is to be direct with clients about what Showcase Designs can improve and what the business must supply.

## Core Rule

No ranking guarantees.

Showcase Designs can improve the website foundation, conversion flow, technical SEO, Google Business Profile alignment, content structure, tracking, and client-facing trust signals. Local search performance still depends on competition, location, relevance, distance, prominence, reviews, fresh photos, accurate business details, links, and real proof of completed work.

If a client cannot or will not provide the required business signals, sell a website foundation or a scoped cleanup project. Do not sell Growth as an ongoing local SEO promise.

## Sales Script

Use plain language:

```text
I can build the website and local SEO foundation, tighten your Google Business Profile alignment, clean up service pages, improve tracking, and make the business look more trustworthy online.

I cannot honestly guarantee rankings. Google local visibility depends on relevance, distance, and prominence. Reviews, fresh photos, accurate business details, service-area proof, and real completed-work proof matter. If those signals are weak or hard to update, we should say that up front and make the first 30-90 days about improving them.
```

## First 30 Days

Showcase Designs handles:

- Website structure, mobile layout, conversion sections, forms, metadata, page titles, internal links, and performance basics.
- Google Business Profile recommendations and website-to-GBP alignment.
- Review request process setup or wording when the client approves it.
- Photo placement, alt text, proof sections, and service-area content structure.
- Analytics-ready event tracking and lead destination checks.

Client handles:

- Review requests to real customers.
- Fresh photos or approved access to take/select photos.
- Accurate business details, service areas, services, hours, licenses, and proof.
- Approval of public claims before launch.
- Access to Google Business Profile, analytics, Search Console, domain, hosting, and email systems when needed.

## Quote Guardrails

- If Google Business Profile access is missing, quote website-first work and list GBP access as a dependency.
- If reviews are stale or weak, include review process setup, but do not promise ranking movement from the website alone.
- If fresh photos are missing, include a photo/proof collection task before or immediately after launch.
- If service areas are unclear, do not build city pages until the client confirms real service coverage and proof.
- If public proof is weak in a competitive trade, keep the promise to foundation, tracking, trust cleanup, and signal improvement.
- If the client will not participate in reviews, photos, proof, or approvals, recommend Starter or a one-time cleanup instead of Growth.

## Do Not Promise

- Guaranteed rankings, map pack placement, traffic, calls, or revenue.
- Review volume the client has not committed to requesting.
- Photos, testimonials, licenses, or proof that the client has not supplied or approved.
- City pages for areas the business does not truly serve.
- "Full SEO" at `$400/mo` unless the monthly scope is capped to reporting, Google Business Profile improvements, review/photo process support, and one priority content/update cycle.

## Client Signoff

Before launch or Growth onboarding, record:

- The client understands there are no ranking guarantees.
- The client understands reviews, fresh photos, accurate business details, service areas, and proof of completed work affect local SEO.
- The client has approved all public claims, project photos, testimonials, service areas, and contact details.
- The client knows which tasks they own during the first 30 days.
